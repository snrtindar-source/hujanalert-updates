HujanAlert Indonesia Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Data we collect

HujanAlert Indonesia (the "App") collects very limited data, only to perform its functions:

  • Device location — only when you allow it, to determine your weather forecast region, show BMKG warnings near you, and position you on the radar map. The App does not continuously track your movement.
  • Region you select — the province/regency/city name you choose manually.
  • Preferences — favorite locations, alarm settings, and display preferences.

2. Data we do NOT collect

  • The App does not ask for your name, email address, or any account.
  • The App does not collect analytics, advertising, or behavioral tracking data.
  • Your GPS coordinates are not sent to any third-party server.

3. Data storage

All data (favorite locations, settings, weather and radar cache) is stored only on your device. No data is sent to third-party servers.

4. Third-party services

The App fetches weather data and warnings from the official public BMKG (Indonesian Meteorological, Climatological, and Geophysical Agency) APIs and open forecast services. Those requests contain only a region code, not your precise device location.

5. Permissions used

  • Location — for forecasts and warnings around you.
  • Notifications — for rain alarms and weather warnings.
  • Internet — to download weather, radar, and tide data.
  • Wake lock — so rain alarms fire on time.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Deleting your data

You can remove favorite locations at any time within the App, or erase all App data via Android Settings (Apps → HujanAlert → Clear Data).

8. Policy changes

Changes to this policy will be announced through App updates. The "Last updated" date above always reflects the latest version.

9. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the official HujanAlert Indonesia channel on Google Play Store (the "Contact us" section on the app page).
